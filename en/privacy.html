Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: October 12, 2026

In short: UnzipX requires no account, serves no ads, does not track you, and does not send selected files, passwords, or usage history to the developer or third-party servers.

1. Scope

This policy applies to UnzipX for macOS. UnzipX compresses and extracts files in locations selected by the user.

2. Data collection

UnzipX does not collect, upload, sell, or share personal data. It includes no advertising, behavioral analytics, or cross-app tracking services.

3. Local file processing

Only files and folders you explicitly select through the system file picker, drag and drop, or Finder services are processed. Compression, extraction, and format detection happen locally on your Mac, and results are written to a destination you choose.

4. Passwords and local settings

  • Archive passwords you choose to save are stored in Apple Keychain.
  • Language, compression options, security-scoped bookmarks, and operation history are stored in the app's local container.
  • Diagnostic logs remain on your Mac for displaying and troubleshooting operation errors and are not uploaded automatically.

5. File permissions

UnzipX uses the macOS App Sandbox. It requests access only to files, folders, or output locations you explicitly select, and uses security-scoped bookmarks to retain access when needed.

6. Third-party components

The app may use bundled archive tools such as 7-Zip and unrar for local file processing. UnzipX does not use these components to transmit your files or personal data over the network. Relevant license notices are included with the app.

7. Your controls and deletion

You can remove saved passwords and history from within the app. Local settings are also removed when you uninstall the app and delete its macOS app container. Because the developer does not hold your personal data, there is no developer-hosted account data to request or delete.

8. Children's privacy

UnzipX does not collect personal information from children and provides no accounts, social features, or advertising.

9. Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices materially change, this page and its effective date will be updated, with notice in release information when appropriate.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, email unzipxSupportService@qq.com.
